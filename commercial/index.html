Food Service Comfort

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air out nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. West Des Moines HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we plan service around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and leaves staff miserable at peak service. Our technicians balance makeup air against hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safe Installation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets an experienced technician dispatched fast, anywhere in West Des Moines and the surrounding area.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on commercial equipment. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling fast. Preventive Care

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment. Every West Des Moines HVAC program is customized to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes using the right MERV rating for the space, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal preparation for peak demand.

Compared with an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses that commit to a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Call us to discuss a plan for your property and budget.
